# PHASE E-09.10: EXAMINATIONS, CBT AND ASSESSMENT GOVERNANCE
## Comprehensive Architectural Specifications, Tenant Capability Mapping, and Implementation Report

---

### Executive Summary

Phase E-09.10 delivers the Super-Admin **Examinations, CBT and Assessment Governance** workspace in the School Management SaaS platform (`dashboard/`). This workspace equips the platform owner with an integrated, privacy-preserving, multi-tenant governance control plane to monitor examination readiness, Computer-Based Testing (CBT) adoption, question-bank taxonomy, timetable scheduling logistics, grading standards, and terminal result publication pipelines across all tenant schools.

In strict accordance with the non-negotiable architectural mandates:
- **Frontend Only:** All changes are strictly confined to `dashboard/`. Zero backend controllers, database schemas, routes, models, or middleware were implemented.
- **Tenant Integrity (Read-Only):** `tenant-dashboard/` was empirically inspected to discover actual capabilities, types, routes, and services, and remains 100% untouched.
- **Privacy by Design & Data Minimization:** Zero student personally identifiable information (PII), individual test answers, raw marks, question text, or answer keys are exposed or ingested into platform-level views. The Super-Admin operates solely on aggregate metrics, anonymized operational telemetry, and readiness indicators.
- **Strict Anti-Hallucination & Evidence Standards:** Every capability status (`implemented`, `partial`, `planned`, `missing`, `unknown`) is justified by direct source file evidence. For instance, tenant CBT utilizes client-side visibility tracking and proctor time extension overrides, but *no* AI webcam biometrics exist; automated proctoring was accurately classified as `missing` (`cap_cbt_auto_proctoring`) rather than claimed.
- **Referential Integrity:** All fixtures and organization data map strictly to platform organization models (`INITIAL_ORGANIZATIONS` `org_001`–`org_010`) and campuses (`INITIAL_CAMPUSES` `camp_001_1`–`camp_010_1`).
- **Module Registry Alignment:** Integrated with the Master Tenant Module Registry (`mod_examinations`, `sub_examinations_*`, `mod_results`, `sub_results_*`).

## 2. Platform Architecture & Implemented Views

The Phase E-09.10 implementation inside `dashboard/` delivers the following production-grade frontend architecture:

### 2.1 Domain Contracts & Types (`dashboard/lib/types/examination-governance.ts`)
- **Assessment Delivery Modes:**
  - `PAPER_BASED`: Traditional paper question papers and answer booklets; physical invigilation.
  - `CBT_HYBRID`: Mixed delivery mode with digital objective testing alongside paper practicals or essays.
  - `CBT_FULL`: 100% digital assessment delivery via the browser CBT engine.
- **Assessment Lifecycle States:**
  - `DRAFT`: Assessment component defined by department head; unapproved.
  - `SCHEDULED`: Timetable slot, venue, and invigilator assigned; students enrolled.
  - `IN_PROGRESS`: Active testing window underway.
  - `MARKS_ENTRY`: Examination concluded; teachers entering or verifying scores.
  - `MODERATION`: Departmental moderation and score normalization.
  - `AWAITING_PUBLICATION`: Results finalized; pending school head sign-off.
  - `PUBLISHED`: Terminal report cards and broadsheets released to parents/students.
  - `CANCELLED`: Series revoked or postponed due to administrative directives.
- **8-Point Canonical Readiness Matrix (`ExamReadinessCheckItem`):**
  1. `active_exam_period`: Designated active examination series with valid date window.
  2. `class_subject_mapping`: Subject-to-class assignments configured with candidate allocations.
  3. `assessment_weighting`: Standardized assessment weighting scheme configured (sums to 100%).
  4. `active_exam_dates`: Non-inverted examination start and end dates.
  5. `timetable_invigilation`: Timetable published with room capacity verification and assigned invigilators.
  6. `grading_scales`: Active grading scale configured with pass marks and performance bands.
  7. `cbt_readiness_or_exemption`: CBT system verified ready, or school certified as paper-based exempt.
  8. `report_card_pipeline`: Terminal report card template and master broadsheet structure configured.
- **Readiness Levels:**
  - `ready`: 0 blockers, score >= 85%.
  - `partially_ready`: <= 1 blocker, score >= 50%.
  - `not_ready`: > 1 blocker or score < 50%.
  - `not_applicable`: School suspended, dormant, or unprovisioned.
- **CBT Telemetry:** Track active sessions, completed attempts, time extensions, browser blur/tab switch flags, connection drops, and battery warnings without recording candidate identities.
- **Timetable Conflicts:** Detect room double-booking, teacher invigilation clashes, and cohort overlaps.

### 2.2 Domain Logic & Evaluator (`dashboard/lib/examination-governance/evaluator.ts`)
- `evaluateExamReadiness`: Evaluates the 8 canonical prerequisite dimensions for any school. Treats paper-based schools (`org_005`) as `not_applicable` for CBT requirements to eliminate false negative blockers.
- `detectExamTimetableConflicts`: Multi-resource clash detector identifying room double-booking and invigilator overlap.
- `filterExamGovernanceData`: Dynamic multi-dimensional filter supporting 9 dimensions simultaneously (Organization, Campus, Academic Period, Assessment Type, Lifecycle Status, Delivery Mode, Readiness Level, Date Range, and Search Query).
- `computeAggregatedExamMetrics`: Pure mathematical rollup ensuring KPIs strictly match the displayed filtered data without hardcoding.
- `getExamCapabilityInventory`: Returns 15 discovered capabilities with source file paths, implementation status, and future backend dependencies.
- `validateExamReferentialIntegrity`: Confirms strict foreign key referential integrity against `INITIAL_ORGANIZATIONS` and `INITIAL_CAMPUSES`.

## 3. Examination Lifecycle Governance & State Mapping

The platform aggregates assessment components across 7 standardized lifecycle stages derived directly from tenant capabilities:

```
[ DRAFT ] 
   └── Component created, weighting defined, awaiting departmental sign-off.
[ SCHEDULED ] 
   └── Room allocated, invigilator assigned, candidate timetable published.
[ IN_PROGRESS ] 
   └── Active examination window underway (CBT sessions or paper hall).
[ MARKS_ENTRY ] 
   └── Testing closed; subject teachers recording raw scores or essay marks.
[ MODERATION ] 
   └── Departmental normalization, standard deviation check, score verification.
[ AWAITING_PUBLICATION ] 
   └── Moderation complete; awaiting principal / head of school sign-off.
[ PUBLISHED ] 
   └── Master broadsheet published; report cards distributed with QR verification.
```

**Governance Boundary:** Super-Admin users have strictly observational and governance oversight. The platform owner cannot alter individual grades, force-submit active candidate tests, or override school publication decisions.

## 4. Privacy, Security, and Anti-Cheating Boundaries

1. **Zero Student PII:** Neither names, student identification numbers, admission numbers, nor guardian contacts are stored or processed in the Super-Admin data fixtures.
2. **Zero Test Content & Answer Keys:** Question text, multiple-choice options, correct solution keys, and candidate written submissions remain strictly encapsulated within tenant databases. The Super-Admin observes only metadata (counts, difficulty distributions, question types, and approval states).
3. **No False Anti-Cheating Claims:** The platform acknowledges that CBT testing relies on human proctors and client-side focus detection. No false claims of "AI automated facial proctoring" are made.
4. **Formula Injection Neutralization:** All CSV exports sanitize text cells to protect spreadsheet software against formula injection attacks.

## 6. Known Gaps and Limitations

1. **Automated AI Proctoring:** Tenant application does not have webcam facial biometrics or audio monitoring. Automated proctoring is marked `missing` (`cap_cbt_auto_proctoring`).
2. **Client-Side Conflict Detection:** Room clash detection is currently simulated on the frontend based on schedule slot intervals. Multi-resource constraint solving should eventually run on the backend.
3. **Multi-Tier Result Moderation:** Tenant has basic score verification; formal multi-step approval workflows require future backend state machines.
