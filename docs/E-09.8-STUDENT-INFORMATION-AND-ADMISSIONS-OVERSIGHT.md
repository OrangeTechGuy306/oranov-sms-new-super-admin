## 1. Executive Summary & Core Constraints

Phase E-09.8 implements the **Student Information & Admissions Oversight Architecture** within the School Management SaaS Super-Admin dashboard (`dashboard/`).

This phase provides platform administrators with a centralized, privacy-conscious macro overview of student lifecycle statuses, admissions funnel conversion rates, multi-branch campus capacity utilization, mobility transfers and clearance operations, and data-quality governance across all tenant organizations.

### Strict Architectural Boundaries Maintained
1. **Frontend-Only Execution:** Backend implementation was strictly paused. Zero backend routes, controllers, services, database schemas, or migrations were created or modified. All computations and aggregations are evaluated deterministically in the client-side data layer.
2. **Tenant Dashboard Protection:** `tenant-dashboard/` remained **strictly read-only**. No tenant-side code or fixtures were altered.
3. **Privacy by Design & Zero PII:** Student information—especially pertaining to minors—is strictly safeguarded. Platform Super-Admin operates purely on aggregate telemetry. No student personal names, dates of birth, residential addresses, contact details, medical records, or guardian dossiers exist in the platform fixtures or exports.
4. **Referential Integrity & Non-Fabrication:** All student statistics and campus allocations are strictly mapped to verified organization IDs (`org_001` through `org_010`) and registered campuses (`camp_001_1` through `camp_010_1`). Unprovisioned or draft schools (e.g., `CEDAR`, `ROYAL`) are explicitly surfaced as unprovisioned rather than synthesizing misleading runtime zeros.
5. **Architectural Reuse:** Integrated seamlessly with existing design systems, Tailwind CSS styling tokens, `StatCard`, `Badge`, `Tabs`, `PageHeader`, `Sidebar`, and formula-injection-safe CSV export utilities.

## 3. Student Oversight Domain Model & Metric Definitions

The domain contracts are established in `dashboard/lib/types/student-oversight.ts` and evaluated by `dashboard/lib/student-oversight/evaluator.ts`.

### 3.1 Metric Definitions & Anti-Conflation Rules

| Metric | Source Field | Definition & Boundary Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Enrolled Students** | `population.enrolledCount` | Currently active learners attending classes (`ACTIVE` or `ENROLLED`). **Must NOT include** admitted applicants, graduated alumni, or transferred students. |
| **Admitted (Pre-Enrolled)** | `population.admittedCount` | Applicants with accepted offers who have not yet commenced classes. |
| **Applications Received** | `admissions.submittedCount` | Formal applicant dossiers submitted for admissions intake in the selected cycle. |
| **Inquiries** | `admissions.inquiryCount` | Preliminary lead inquiries and prospective family contacts. **Not counted as formal applications.** |
| **Awaiting Review** | `admissions.underReviewCount` + `moreInfoCount` | Applications currently in reviewer triage or awaiting applicant document resubmission. |
| **Accepted Offers** | `admissions.acceptedCount` | Applications approved by the school's admissions committee. |
| **Completed Enrollments** | `admissions.convertedCount` | Accepted applicants who finalized tuition registration, were assigned an admission number, and matriculated. |
| **Conversion Rate (%)** | `admissions.conversionRatePercentage` | `Math.round((convertedCount / submittedCount) * 100)`. Distinguishes inquiries from submitted conversion. |
| **Physical Campus Capacity** | `campuses[].studentCapacity` | Configured maximum learner seating/facility capacity per campus branch. |
| **Capacity Utilization (%)** | `campuses[].capacityUtilizationPercentage` | `Math.round((enrolledStudents / studentCapacity) * 100)`. Values > 100% trigger automated `OVER_CAPACITY` audit warnings. |
| **Outgoing Transfers** | `transfers.outgoingTransfersTotal` | Departure requests undergoing multi-department clearance or completed transfer out. |
| **Clearance Bottlenecks** | `transfers.clearanceBottlenecks[]` | Pending and blocked items broken down by department: `LIBRARY`, `HOSTEL`, `ASSETS`, `FINANCE`. |
| **Promoted Students** | `promotions.promotedCount` | Students evaluated and advanced to the next academic level under academic continuity policy. |
| **Graduated Alumni** | `population.graduatedCount` | Completed final graduation cohort (e.g., Grade 12). Tracked as alumni records; **strictly excluded from active enrollment totals.** |
| **Data Quality Issues** | `dataQualityIssues[]` | Derived anomalies: `MISSING_SESSION`, `UNASSIGNED_CAMPUS`, `OVER_CAPACITY`, `DOCS_PENDING_EXCESSIVE`, `UNMAPPED_STATUS`, `MODULE_DISABLED`. |

## 4. Workspaces & User Experience Architecture

The dedicated workspace is located at route `/student-oversight` and integrated into the Super-Admin sidebar navigation (`dashboard/components/layout/Sidebar.tsx`).

### 4.1 Filter Architecture (`StudentOversightFilters.tsx`)
- **Search Query:** Real-time filter across organization names and alphanumeric codes (e.g. `OAKHILL`, `STJUDE`).
- **Organization Selector:** Dropdown of all registered organizations in `PlatformStore`. Selecting an organization automatically constrains available campus options.
- **Context-Aware Campus Selector:** Dynamically resolves selectable campuses based on the active organization selection.
- **Academic Cycle / Period Selector:** Supports `2025/2026` (Current Cycle), `2024/2025` (Previous Cycle), `2023/2024` (Historical), and `ALL`.
- **Reporting Scope Toggles:** `All Tenants`, `Active Reporting Only`, and `With Quality Warnings`.
- **CSV Export Action:** One-click download of RFC 4180 compliant CSV reflecting the active filtered view.
- **Filter Reset:** Restores baseline view with a single click.

### 4.2 Tabbed Workspaces
1. **Platform Overview (`overview`):**
   - 6 Top-level StatCards (Enrolled Students, Applications Received, Awaiting Review, Transfers, Reporting Tenants, Data Quality Alerts).
   - Side-by-side Admissions Funnel (`AdmissionsFunnelCard`) and Population Lifecycle Composition (`StudentPopulationDistributionCard`).
   - Cross-Tenant Organization Summary Table (`OrganizationStudentTable`).
   - Automated Data Quality Audit Notice Banner (`DataQualityAuditBanner`).
2. **Admissions & Intake Funnel (`admissions`):**
   - Full pipeline breakdown: Inquiries ➔ Applications ➔ Review ➔ Accepted Offers ➔ Completed Enrollments.
   - Separate terminal dispositions for Deferred admissions and Ineligible/Rejected applicants.
   - Conversion throughput rates per organization.
3. **Campuses & Facility Capacity (`campuses`):**
   - Campus-level breakdown with physical facility capacity, active enrolled students, capacity utilization progress bars, and over-capacity warnings.
4. **Transfers & Academic Continuity (`transfers_promotions`):**
   - Student mobility tracking (outgoing clearances vs incoming transfer credit reviews).
   - Multi-department clearance bottlenecks breakdown (`LIBRARY`, `HOSTEL`, `ASSETS`, `FINANCE`).
   - Year-end academic promotion outcomes (Promoted, On Trial, Repeat Year, Graduated Cohort).
   - Administrative safety notice affirming tenant operational autonomy.
5. **Data Quality & Governance (`data_governance`):**
   - Central audit panel highlighting active compliance and data anomalies across all tenants.
   - Referential integrity and privacy-by-design verification rules.

### 4.3 Organization Drill-Down Modal (`OrganizationStudentDetailModal.tsx`)
Clicking "Drill-down" on any organization opens a detailed inspection modal:
- Shows organization classification, lifecycle status, reporting state, and last reported timestamp.
- Displays prominent Privacy by Design badge reminding administrators that telemetry is aggregated.
- Summarizes campuses, admissions progression stages, academic division distributions, department clearance statuses, and data quality notices for that specific school.
- Zero individual student PII is displayed or requested.

## 5. Privacy by Design & Data Minimization Analysis

| Question | Assessment & Decision |
|---|---|
| **Are student names, DOBs, or contacts needed for platform oversight?** | **NO.** Platform administrators require macro-level capacity, enrollment trends, admissions conversion, and billing reconciliation. Individual student identities are irrelevant and hazardous to store centrally. |
| **Can platform goals be met with aggregated telemetry?** | **YES.** All platform oversight objectives (capacity alerts, conversion funnels, tenant health scoring) are fully satisfied by organization-level and campus-level counters. |
| **Does client-side filtering constitute access control?** | **NO.** Frontend filters are presentation utilities. The Super-Admin application defaults to an aggregate-only data model so that individual student records are never fetched from the server into the browser. |
| **How are demonstration records handled?** | Demonstration fixtures are strictly non-identifiable and organization-scoped. No real student personal details exist anywhere in the codebase. |

## 6. Export Utilities & Formula Injection Defense

The export utility is implemented in `dashboard/lib/utils/student-oversight-export.ts`:
- **Active Filter Adherence:** Exports exactly the records matching the current user-selected filters.
- **Zero Student PII:** Contains only aggregated tenant fields (Organization Code, Classification, Enrolled Students, Inquiries, Admissions Received, Conversion Rate, Campuses, Quality Issues).
- **Formula Injection Defense:** Uses `generateCsvContent` from `dashboard/lib/utils/csv-export.ts`, sanitizing cells starting with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, `\t`, or `\r` by prefixing a single quote `'` and escaping double quotes (RFC 4180).
- **Automated Timestamping:** Output filename encodes the date and active session filter (e.g., `student-oversight-report-2025-2026-2026-10-17.csv`).

## 8. Known Gaps & Limitations

1. **Backend Integration:** In compliance with Phase E-09.8 constraints, no backend routes or database tables were created. Student oversight metrics currently read from canonical client-side fixtures (`INITIAL_STUDENT_OVERSIGHT_DATA`).
2. **Real-Time Telemetry Streaming:** Live WebSocket updates from tenant school deployments require future backend event-bus ingestion (documented in `E-09.8-BACKEND-READINESS.md`).
3. **Cross-Tenant Benchmarking:** Cross-school percentiles (e.g., standard deviations of conversion rates) require future differential privacy aggregators to prevent small-cohort inference.
4. **Historical Session Depth:** Fixtures currently provide 3 sessions (`2025/2026`, `2024/2025`, `2023/2024`). Deeper multi-decade archiving requires cloud cold-storage indexing.
