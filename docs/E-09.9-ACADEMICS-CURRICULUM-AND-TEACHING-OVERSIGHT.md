# PHASE E-09.9: ACADEMICS, CURRICULUM & TEACHING OVERSIGHT
## Comprehensive Architectural Specifications, Tenant Capability Mapping, and Implementation Report

---

### Executive Summary

Phase E-09.9 establishes the Super-Admin **Academics, Curriculum & Teaching Oversight** workspace in the School Management SaaS platform (`dashboard/`). This workspace provides platform administrators with a centralized, privacy-conscious, multi-tenant overview of academic structures, term calendars, curriculum blueprints, schemes of work, timetables, instructional delivery workflows, and reporting readiness across schools.

In strict adherence to architectural directives:
- **Zero Backend Alterations:** No backend models, routes, or databases were created or modified.
- **Tenant Integrity (Read-Only):** `tenant-dashboard/` was inspected for empirical discovery and remains 100% untouched.
- **Privacy by Design & Data Minimization:** Zero student personally identifiable information (PII), zero student marks/exam scores, and zero staff contact details are exposed. Platform administrators observe strictly aggregated indicators, readiness scores, and conflict analyses.
- **Referential Integrity:** All organization models resolve strictly to `INITIAL_ORGANIZATIONS` (`org_001` - `org_010`) and `INITIAL_CAMPUSES` (`camp_001_1` - `camp_010_1`).
- **Module Registry Integration:** Academic capabilities connect directly to the Master Tenant Module Registry established in Phase E-09.6 (`mod_academics`, `mod_timetables`, `mod_examinations`, `sub_academics_curriculum`, etc.).

## 2. Platform Architecture & Implemented Views

The Phase E-09.9 implementation in `dashboard/` consists of:

### 2.1 Domain Contracts & Types (`dashboard/lib/types/academic-oversight.ts`)
- `OrganizationAcademicOversight`: Canonical domain contract integrating session calendar, departmental structures, curriculum blueprints, timetables, pedagogical workflows, assessment readiness, and data quality issues.
- `AcademicYearConfigSummary` & `AcademicPeriodSummary`: Typed models for academic years and period breakdowns with date validity tracking (`'valid'`, `'invalid_order'`, `'overlapping_periods'`).
- `CurriculumOversightSummary`: Tracks curriculum frameworks (`NERDC`, `CAMBRIDGE_IGCSE`, `BRITISH_NATIONAL`, `IB_MYP`, `AMERICAN_COMMON_CORE`, `CUSTOM`) and scheme approval pipelines.
- `TimetableOversightSummary` & `TimetableConflictItem`: Multi-dimensional conflict tracking across teacher double-bookings, room clashes, and class cohort overlaps.
- `PedagogicalWorkflowsSummary`: Aggregate indicators of lesson note authoring, HOD approvals, and daily lesson delivery tracking.
- `AssessmentReportingSummary`: Scale types (`PERCENTAGE`, `LETTER_GRADE`, `GPA_4`, `GPA_5`, `CUSTOM`, `NONE`), pass marks, report card template readiness, and broadsheet generation readiness.
- `AcademicDataQualityIssue`: Typed governance audit issues with severity badges and suggested remediation steps.

### 2.2 Evaluator & Domain Logic (`dashboard/lib/academic-oversight/evaluator.ts`)
- `validateAcademicYearPeriods`: Validates chronological order (`startDate < endDate`), period boundary inclusion, and detects overlapping period dates.
- `evaluateAcademicReadiness`: Evaluates an 8-point canonical checklist (Active session, designated current period, class & subject configuration, >=80% teacher allocation, approved schemes, published timetable without critical conflicts, active grading scale, report card template). Computes readiness score (0-100%) and level (`complete`, `attention_required`, `critical_blocker`, `unprovisioned`).
- `detectTimetableConflicts`: Multi-resource conflict detector evaluating teacher overlaps, room clashes, and class cohort double-bookings.
- `filterAcademicOversightData`: Dynamic filtering engine supporting 7 simultaneous dimensions (Organization, Campus, Academic Session, Active Period, Readiness Level, Capability Category, and full-text Search).
- `computeAggregatedAcademicMetrics`: Mathematically sound roll-up metrics ensuring summary cards reflect the exact underlying data without hardcoding or fabrication.
- `getAcademicCapabilityInventory`: Verified inventory of 16 capabilities linked directly to the E-09.6 module registry.
- `validateReferentialIntegrity`: Confirms strict foreign key mapping to `INITIAL_ORGANIZATIONS` and `INITIAL_CAMPUSES`.

## 3. Data Integrity, Privacy by Design & Governance

| Governance Principle | Implementation Verification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Zero Student PII** | Unit test verifies zero student names, admission numbers, emails, addresses, or DOB exist anywhere in fixtures. |
| **Zero Assessment Marks** | Zero raw test scores, exam marks, or individual report-card results are exposed; only aggregated scale types and publication readiness are visible. |
| **Tenant Autonomy** | Super-Admin views configuration completeness and conflict alerts without modifying tenant policies or approving lesson plans remotely. |
| **Referential Integrity** | 100% of organization IDs map to `INITIAL_ORGANIZATIONS`; 100% of campus IDs map to `INITIAL_CAMPUSES`. |
| **Formula Injection Defense** | CSV export prepends `'` to cells beginning with `=, +, -, @` to neutralize spreadsheet DDE formula execution. |
| **Deterministic Data** | Summary metrics are computed from canonical record rollups; unknown states are strictly distinguished from true zeroes. |
